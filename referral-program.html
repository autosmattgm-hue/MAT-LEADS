Important program details

Who can participate?

MAT LEADS members can get a personal referral link from the Earn Hub. Rewards are recorded against qualifying referrals attributed to that link.

How much can I earn?

The current reward is $0.20 for an eligible registration and $4.00 for an eligible subscription. These are fixed program rewards, not a promise of income.

When can I request a payout?

The current minimum withdrawal is $50. Rewards remain subject to eligibility checks and payout review; see your Earn Hub for your actual referral status.
