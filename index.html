What is MAT LEADS AI PRO X?

It is a global lead-generation and client-acquisition workspace. You can discover businesses, review real opportunity signals, manage follow-ups, prepare outreach, audit websites, and create websites from one account.

Can I search outside my home country?

Yes. Search filters are designed for markets worldwide. Availability and contact coverage depend on the underlying data providers and the location you choose.

Does the AI send messages automatically?

No. AI drafts are editable suggestions. You remain in control of what is sent, where it is sent, and when a follow-up is recorded.

Can I export and manage my own records?

Yes. The workspace supports saved leads, pipeline stages, notes, follow-ups, and export workflows so your business data stays useful beyond one search.
